A friend invites you to Spin Fish

Spin Fish is a spinning fishing game on Ukrainian waters. Install the game and enter your friend’s code: you become friends in the game at once. When you reach level 5, you both get 250 Scales and a pack of lures.

Friend’s code——————

Enter the code in your first 3 days: Profile → “Have a friend’s code?”

The link has no code. Ask your friend to send it again: in the game, Profile → “Invite a friend”.

App Store Google Play

  2. 2Open Profile → “Have a friend’s code?” and enter the code.
