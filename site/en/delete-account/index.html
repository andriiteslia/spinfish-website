Delete your account

Spin Fish · Andrii Teslia

You can delete your Spin Fish account with all its data at any time — both a guest account and one signed in with Apple or Google.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Spin Fish and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately. After that you can delete the game from your phone to remove the settings stored on the device.

The “Delete account” button arrives in the game together with Apple and Google sign-in. If your version does not have it yet, write to us as described below.

Without the game — by email

Write to support@spinfish.app with the subject “Delete my account”.

  • If you signed in with Apple or Google, write from that account’s email or mention it in the message so we can find your account.
  • If you played as a guest and have already deleted the game, your data is not linked to a name or email, so we cannot tell which data is yours.
  • We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We never ask for a password.

We answer within 30 days, usually within a few days, and let you know when the account is deleted.

What is deleted

  • The account and its identifier, linked Apple and Google sign-ins (email and name, if we received them).
  • All progress: level, experience, waters, tasks, streak, karma, records, catch.
  • Scales and their journal, lures, tackle and kits.
  • Records of in-game purchases. Purchased Scales are deleted too, and the money is not refunded (refunds only through Apple or Google under their rules).

What remains

  • Settings on your phone — until you delete the game.
  • Database backups — up to 7 days, then they expire on their own.
  • Messages you sent to support — up to 24 months (we can delete them sooner on request).
  • Your Apple or Google account is not affected. You can separately remove the game’s access in your Apple ID or Google account settings.
