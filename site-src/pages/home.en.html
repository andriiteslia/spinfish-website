Spinning fishing in your pocket

Swipe to cast, work the lure, strike and play the fish. Ten Ukrainian waters, from a village pond to the Danube and the Black Sea.

Like real fishing, only in your pocket

Cast and retrieve

Swipe up to cast. Near or far, onto the shallows or over a deep hole — it decides who bites. The reel lever works the lure slowly, fast or in jerks.

Fight the fish

The strike, line tension, the drag. A big zander pulls down, a pike dashes sideways and can bite through the fluoro leader, and a big fish at the shore still has to go into the landing net.

10 waters, 19 species

Prylbychi, Berezivka, the Dniester, the Dnipro, Chornyi Omut, Basivka, the Desna, the Carpathians, the Danube and the Black Sea. Perch, pike, zander, catfish, asp, trout and many more.

Dawn, night and weather

A day in the game lasts an hour. Fish bite better at dawn, catfish and zander come out at night — light up the water with your torch. Rain, wind and pressure change the bite, and a strong wind affects how accurately you cast.

Lures and tackle

15 lures in different sizes and jig weights, rods, reels, braids and monofilament. Every piece affects the result: the distance, the bites, which fish and how big.

Levels and tasks

100 levels and 10 ranks, new waters that open at the right level, daily tasks, a fishing-day streak and a fresh bite every day.

Leaderboards

For the week and for all time: catch, the biggest fish of each species, level, karma. You can see who else is fishing right now.

Angler’s karma

Release small fish and Red Book species — and the water pays you back with trophies.

Your progress is safe

Play right away as a guest, and if you sign in with Apple or Google, your progress is saved on the server and moves to a new phone.

How it looks

For now, the game’s language is Ukrainian only.
