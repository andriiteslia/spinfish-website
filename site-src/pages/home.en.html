Like real fishing, only in your pocket

Cast and retrieve

Swipe up to cast. Near or far, into the weeds or over a deep hole — it decides who bites. The reel lever works the lure slowly, fast or in jerks.

Fight the fish

The strike, line tension, the drag. A big zander pulls down, a pike dashes sideways, and snags and break-offs are part of the game.

8 waters, 19 species

From the Prylbychi pond and Berezivka to the Dniester, the Dnipro, the Carpathians and the Black Sea. Perch, pike, zander, catfish, asp, trout and many more.

Lures and tackle

15 lures in different sizes and jig weights, rods, reels, braids and monofilament. Every piece changes something: the distance, the bites, which fish and how big.

Levels and leaderboards

100 levels and 10 ranks, daily tasks and a day streak. Leaderboards for the week and for all time: catch, the biggest fish of each species, level, karma.

Angler’s karma

Release small fish and Red Book species — and the water pays you back with trophies.

How it looks

For now, the game’s language is Ukrainian only.
